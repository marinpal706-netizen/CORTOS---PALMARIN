# Surface brief: PLMRN portal (home, short page, about and supporting pages)

## Scope

- Mode: experience (the films lead from the first viewport). The admin page inherits the system quietly, in operate mode.
- Audience: fans of the channel (often on mobile, arriving from a shared link) and festival programmers/collaborators, weighted equally.
- Job: watch the shorts, rate them, comment and save favourites. Programmers judge the studio from the short pages, the laurels and the contact details.
- Constraints:
  - Every current function stays.
  - Free to watch.
  - Multi-genre.
  - Two colours only: night blue and white.
  - Rounded and modern.
  - Not minimalist, not cold.
- Reference the owner supplied: a Spanish streaming portal for short films. It has a full-bleed hero carousel with a "Ver ahora" pill, genre rows of 16:9 tiles with the title over the image, a dark navy ground, white type and pill buttons. Its craft level is the bar. Do not copy its brand, logo or copy.
- Unresolved: real clip files. For now the clips are the shorts' own YouTube trailers or films, playing muted in the background.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard played straight. PLMRN as a streaming portal of its own shorts: watch first, browse by mood, with the studio's identity carried by the quality of the frames. This is the canon path the owner chose, with their example as the bar.

OWN-WORLD:
- Ground and colour: a night-blue ground (#0b1018) with layered navy surfaces, and white as the only accent. Text, buttons, active states, stars and hearts are all white.
- Shape: rounded tiles (12px), pill buttons and soft panels (18px).
- Type: Figtree, bold for headings.
- Imagery: the shorts' own frames, full-bleed under gradients.
- Exclusions: no genre colours and no decorative glyphs.

STORY: The visitor lands on a short already playing, presses "Ver ahora" or swipes to the next one, then browses rows by mood (recent, best rated, terror, suspense…), then searches the full catalogue. The programmer finds the short page, laurels and contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- A full-bleed hero carousel of the five most recent shorts.
- Each slide shows the thumbnail first, then the trailer playing muted and looping behind a bottom-left gradient.
- The title is large and bottom-left, with a meta line (year · genre · rating) and two lines of synopsis.
- "Ver ahora" (a white pill) and "Más información" sit below.
- Round prev/next arrows sit at the sides, with dots and a pause control at the bottom.
- A sticky navy header carries the PLMRN wordmark, the nav, search and an "Entrar" pill.

FORM: The streaming portal (canon card). Signature interaction: the hero clip, where the trailer fades in behind the title a moment after the slide appears and stops when the slide leaves. Motion: soft crossfades between slides, tiles that lift on hover, and everything off under reduced motion. seed: none (pinned) — owner, in chat: "estilo de plataforma de streaming", "que al inicio aparezcan clips de los cortos", "azul noche + blanco o parecido", "más actual más redondo"

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
